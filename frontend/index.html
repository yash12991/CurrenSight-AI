About CurrenSight AI

Empowering Financial Independence Through Computer Vision

🌟 Our Mission

CurrenSight AI is an assistive computer vision system created to empower blind and visually impaired individuals in India to independently and confidently identify Indian currency banknotes in real time.

🧠 AI & Computer Vision Architecture

Powered by a hybrid vision pipeline combining deep learning and classic computer vision:

  • ResNet-18 Deep Neural Network: Real-time denomination classification across ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, and ₹2000 notes.
  • Contour & Edge Localization: Real-time banknote boundary detection with dynamic bounding-box overlays.
  • Color & Texture Guardrails: HSV histogram and Laplacian variance checks to distinguish genuine currency characteristics from untextured sketches.
  • Sub-10ms Inference: Optimized on CPU and GPU pipelines running at over 120 FPS.

♿ Accessibility First (WCAG 2.1 AA)

  • Voice Synthesis: Automatic, natural spoken audio output via the browser Web Speech API.
  • High-Contrast Mode: Specially calibrated ultra-high contrast palette (Pure Black & Bright Yellow) for low-vision users.
  • Full Keyboard & Tactile Control: Dedicated keyboard hotkeys (Alt+C, Alt+V, Alt+A, Space) and touch gestures.
